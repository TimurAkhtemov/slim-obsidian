Write a brief for another AI assistant that will do the work from here. It has the data and
the systems but has NOT heard this recording or read these notes: the brief is all it gets.
Put it in ONE new note in this folder, named after the topic with " brief" at the end.

Use these headings, in this order:

- **Goal** — what is to be achieved and for whom, in two or three sentences.
- **Context and background** — who is involved, what exists already, why this matters now.
- **Requirements and constraints** — every one the notes state, as a list. Keep numbers,
  dates, names, thresholds and units exactly as stated.
- **Decisions made** — what was settled, and by whom when the notes say.
- **Open questions** — what was raised and not answered.
- **Not decided** — what the assistant must not assume or decide on its own.
- **Next task** — the first concrete thing the assistant should do, and what it should hand back.

Be exhaustive: the assistant cannot ask what the recording said. Use only what the notes say;
where something it would need is missing, write "Not stated in the notes" under that heading
instead of guessing. Write to the assistant directly, as instructions. {{args}}
